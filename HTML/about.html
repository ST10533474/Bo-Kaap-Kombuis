About Bo-Kaap Kombuis

Discover our story and cultural heritage.

Our Story

Bo-Kaap Kombuis is a restaurant located in the historic Bo-Kaap area of Cape Town. The restaurant celebrates Cape Malay cuisine and the cultural heritage of the area.

The restaurant provides visitors with an opportunity to experience traditional flavours while learning more about the cultural traditions associated with Cape Malay cooking.

Our Culture

Food is an important part of Cape Malay culture. Traditional spices, cooking methods and recipes have been passed down through generations and continue to form an important part of the Bo-Kaap's identity.
